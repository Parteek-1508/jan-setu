
# Jan Setu 🇮🇳

### A Digital Bridge Between Citizens and Government Services

Jan Setu is a citizen-focused digital platform designed to make government services easier to discover, understand, and access.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔎 **Find Government Schemes**  
  Discover relevant government schemes and benefits.

- 📢 **Report Civic Issues**  
  Report problems such as roads, street lights, and other local civic issues.

- 📍 **Complaint Tracking**  
  Track the status of a reported issue until it is resolved.

- 📄 **Document Helper**  
  Find out which documents are required for different government services.

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of Jan Setu is to simplify the interaction between citizens and government services through one easy-to-use platform.

## 💻 Project

This repository contains the prototype and presentation of the Jan Setu project.
